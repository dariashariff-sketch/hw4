# Campus Customs: Design Note (Problem 10)

## What we changed
**1. An interactive dorm-room home page.** Inspired by Gucci's Gift Giving rooms, the site now opens on an illustrated Yale dorm room at night: string lights, a gothic window looking out on New Haven, and a YALE pennant on the wall. Every object is a doorway into the shop:

| Object | Opens |
|---|---|
| Hoodie on the desk chair | Hoodies |
| Crewnecks stacked on the dresser | Crewnecks |
| Tees on the shelf | T-Shirts |
| Quarter-zip on the hook | Quarter-Zips |
| Fleece jacket hanging on the dresser | Jackets & Fleece |
| Laptop | All products |
| Pennant | About Us |
| Bulldog | The shopping assistant (chat) |

- The room shifts in layers as you move the mouse.
- Pulsing white markers and a soft white hover glow with a label ("Shop hoodies") show what's clickable.
- Clicking zooms the camera into the object, then opens that section.
- "Start with the hoodie" and "Skip to the shop" buttons are always visible.

**2. A "Yale at night" look across the whole site.**
- **Palette:** midnight and Yale navy, cream paper, and tones of white. **No yellow or gold anywhere**, for a clean, minimalist look.
- **Type:** an editorial serif for headings, a clean sans for everything else.
- **Layout:** numbered sections, an uneven "lookbook" of featured products, a big "Boola *Boola.*" footer.
- **Motion:** pages fade in, sections slide in as you scroll, and cards lift on hover.
- **Top bar:** white over the dorm room, dark navy on the other pages.

**3. Clean product photos.** 76 of the 102 photos had black backgrounds or black side bars. They now all sit on clean white with smooth edges. The original files are untouched.

**4. Less clutter.**
- No emojis anywhere, including the assistant's replies.
- Icons only where shoppers expect them: cart, account, menu, close, chat, back, and the added-to-cart check.
- No insider shorthand: the chat button says "Chat with us", not "Ask CC".

## Why it helps customers click around and buy
- **Browsing feels like play, not homework.** Instead of a wall of links, shoppers explore a room that feels like their own. Curiosity ("what's on the bed?") turns into clicks, and every click lands on real products.
- **The best seller is the first thing you see.** The glowing hoodie on the chair is the obvious first click, and it goes straight to the category most shoppers want.
- **It never gets in the way.** Shoppers who already know what they want can use "Skip to the shop", the regular top-bar links, or the chat. All of them stay one click away. On phones, the whole room still fits and the buttons sit just below it.
- **Clear signals about what to do next.** Markers, glows, and labels show what's clickable. Actions are solid navy (Add to Cart) or crisp white with a navy outline (Buy Now, Checkout, Start with the hoodie), so the next step stands out without loud colors.
- **Products look like they're worth buying.** Clean white photos, generous spacing, and the editorial type make the gear look premium and trustworthy instead of thrown together. That helps people commit to Add to Cart.
- **Calm, readable pages.** With no emojis or decorative icons, prices, sizes, and stock (the information that decides a purchase) are easier to scan.
- **It's memorable.** A distinctive, on-brand experience makes Campus Customs stick in people's minds, so they're more likely to come back and to share it.
- **It works for everyone.** Every dorm-room object can be reached with the keyboard (Tab and Enter), the animations switch off for people who turn on reduced motion, and every page still has plain links to every section.
